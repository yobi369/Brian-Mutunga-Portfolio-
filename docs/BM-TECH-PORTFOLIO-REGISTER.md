## Decision rules

- **Pilot / evaluation:** A customer-facing demo or scoped pilot may be discussed, subject to written scope, customer-specific checks and explicit limitations.
- **Early access:** Useful engineering exists, but material validation, deployment or operating requirements remain.
- **Research / development:** Do not market as a production product or accept critical customer workloads.
- **Internal / archive:** Retain for engineering learning or review; not part of the public sales catalogue.
- A repository being public does not grant BM Tech exclusive rights. Check each repository's current licence, historical grants, third-party dependencies, trademarks and customer obligations before licensing or selling it.
- Product status must be reviewed against the current default branch and deployment before any public claim changes.
