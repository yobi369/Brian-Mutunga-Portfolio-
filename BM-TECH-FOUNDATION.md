# BM Tech — Company Foundation

> Working business foundation for the transition from a personal engineering portfolio to a technology company. This document describes the intended positioning and current direction; it does not claim that BM Tech is a registered legal entity.

## 1. Company positioning

**BM Tech — Technology Products, Software Solutions & Digital Infrastructure**

BM Tech is being developed as a technology company serving customers in Kenya and internationally. It will build and maintain its own software products while providing selected technology services to businesses, institutions, and individuals.

The company should be presented as a product-led technology business with a services capability—not only as a freelance web-development agency.

### Positioning statement

BM Tech builds practical software products and delivers dependable technology solutions that help organisations operate, connect, protect, and grow.

### Operating principles

- Solve a defined customer problem before adding features.
- Be transparent about what is live, in pilot, experimental, or planned.
- Treat security, data integrity, accessibility, and maintainability as engineering requirements.
- Prefer affordable, supportable solutions and clear ownership.
- Build for Kenyan operating realities while keeping interfaces and delivery suitable for international customers.

## 2. Business structure

### A. BM Tech Products

Software products owned and maintained by BM Tech. Each product should eventually have its own product page, demo or screenshots, documentation, pricing, onboarding path, support route, and clearly stated readiness status.

Initial portfolio candidates:

| Product | Focus | Current commercial treatment |
|---|---|---|
| Precious SchoolBooks | School finance and administration software | Flagship candidate; controlled pilot/readiness work. Not cleared for live school financial data until its acceptance and production-readiness gates pass. |
| Secure Properties | Real-estate technology and property workflows | Product candidate; validate customer demand and operational requirements before making launch claims. |
| PacketWhisperer AI | Network-traffic analysis and security workflows | Technical product candidate; validate scope, security boundaries, hosting, and support model before commercial release. |

Other repositories may be presented as engineering projects, research, prototypes, or future product candidates. A repository is not automatically a launched or supported product.

### B. BM Tech Services

Services should be scoped and quoted per customer requirement. Initial service categories:

1. **Business websites and web applications** — responsive websites, portals, internal tools, and customer-facing applications.
2. **Backend systems and integrations** — APIs, databases, workflow automation, and system integrations.
3. **IT systems and infrastructure** — technical assessment, network planning, system setup, documentation, and support within agreed scope.
4. **Cybersecurity foundations** — security-conscious application reviews, configuration reviews, defensive tooling, and practical risk-reduction guidance within demonstrated capability.

Do not advertise certifications, compliance guarantees, penetration-testing credentials, service-level commitments, or 24/7 support unless they are independently established and operationally available.

## 3. Target markets

- **Kenya:** SMEs, schools, property businesses, and organisations that need practical software or technology support.
- **International:** customers seeking remote software development, product access, integrations, and technical consulting.

Local and international offers may use different pricing, payment, tax, support, and contracting arrangements. These must be defined before accepting paid engagements.

## 4. Customer journey

1. Customer discovers BM Tech through the company website, referrals, professional networks, or product demonstrations.
2. Customer selects a product or describes a service requirement.
3. BM Tech confirms fit, scope, limitations, timeline, and commercial terms.
4. Customer receives a written quotation or product subscription proposal.
5. Work or onboarding begins only after the required agreement and payment terms are confirmed.
6. Delivery includes documentation, handover, and a defined support route.

## 5. Website direction

The existing portfolio should evolve into the BM Tech company website while retaining its distinctive editorial visual language and accurate engineering case studies.

Recommended primary navigation:

- Home
- Products
- Services
- Engineering / About
- Contact

The website should distinguish:
- available services from planned services;
- live products from pilots and prototypes;
- product demonstrations from production systems;
- source repositories from customer-facing product experiences.

The founder profile remains important, but it should support the company story rather than dominate the homepage.

## 6. Commercial foundation still required

Before public commercial launch, define and verify:

- Official business/legal status and the exact name under which contracts and invoices will be issued.
- Business contact email, phone/WhatsApp channel, and customer support ownership.
- Domain and hosting arrangements.
- Service scope, exclusions, delivery process, and quotation template.
- Product pricing, licensing, payment collection, refunds, renewals, and support terms.
- Privacy notice, terms of service, data retention, and customer-data handling.
- Customer onboarding, issue reporting, and escalation process.

Until these are in place, do not imply formal registration, enterprise certifications, existing customers, guaranteed uptime, or contractual support coverage.

## 7. Phased execution

### Phase 1 — Establish BM Tech
Confirm positioning, company identity, contact channels, legal/commercial facts, and website information architecture.

### Phase 2 — Convert the portfolio
Rework the current personal portfolio into a company website with clear Products and Services paths while preserving accurate project case studies.

### Phase 3 — Prepare commercial operations
Create service packages and quotation materials, product pages, enquiry handling, privacy/terms drafts, and customer onboarding.

### Phase 4 — Prepare the flagship product
Resume Precious SchoolBooks launch preparation only after the BM Tech foundation is coherent. Its own production-readiness and school-acceptance gates remain mandatory.

## 8. Brand voice

Clear, capable, grounded, and technically honest. Avoid inflated claims, generic AI language, vague promises, and jargon that obscures what the customer receives.

**Short description:** BM Tech builds software products and delivers practical technology solutions for organisations in Kenya and beyond.
